Learning Objectives

To understand the basic concepts in Probability Theory and Statistics through practical examples.

Course Description

The course is divided into two parts: In first part, basic concepts of probability theory are introduced. In the second part, different problems in classical statistics are discussed.

Course Outline

# Topic (from syllabus) Importance
1 Conditional probability, Bayes' rule, Total probability law, Independence of events High
2 Random variables (discrete and continuous), PMF, PDF, Expectation, variance, moments, CDF High
3 Function of random variables; Multiple random variables, joint and marginal, conditioning and independence High
4 Markov and Chebyshev inequalities; Different notions of convergence Medium
5 Weak law of large numbers, Central limit theorem High
6 Estimation: Unbiased Estimator, MVUE, Rao-Cramer Inequality, Maximum Likelihood Estimator, Efficiency, MSE High
7 Confidence Interval: Coverage Probability, Confidence level, Sample size determination High
8 Testing of Hypotheses: Null and Alternative Hypotheses, Test Statistic, Error Probabilities, Power Function, Level of Significance, Neyman-Pearson Lemma High

Learning Outcomes

Students will become familiar with principal concepts of probability theory and statistics. This helps them to handle, mathematically, various practical problems arising in uncertain situations.
