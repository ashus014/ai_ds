EMC 5103

Probability and Statistics

Semester 1 · Core Course

Overview

Mathematical foundations of probability theory and statistical inference — essential groundwork for machine learning, data science, and research.

Topics

  • Probability axioms, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem
  • Random variables — discrete and continuous distributions
  • Expectation, variance, and moment generating functions
  • Estimation — MLE, method of moments, confidence intervals
  • Hypothesis testing and regression analysis
